A fraud ring here means the local transaction neighbourhood around one or more nodes the model flags as likely illicit: starting from the gold seed node(s), the graph expands outward 2 hops over real tdt edges (directed Bitcoin transactions — arrows point from payer to payee), in both directions, so you see money flowing both into and out of the seed. Coordinated fraud tends to show up structurally as a seed transacting with a cluster of other illicit-looking nodes rather than in isolation — that clustering is what this view is meant to make visible.
